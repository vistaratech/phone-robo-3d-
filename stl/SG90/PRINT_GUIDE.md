# PhoneBot - SG90 Servo Print Guide

All STL files in this folder are modified/verified for **SG90 micro servo** compatibility.
Just open all files in your slicer and print!

## Hardware

- **8x SG90 Servo Motors**
- M3 & M2.5 heat-set inserts
- M2 & M2.5 screws

## Print Settings (Recommended)

- Material: PLA or PETG
- Layer height: 0.2mm
- Walls: 3-4 perimeters
- Infill: 40-60%
- Supports: Yes (for U-shaped cradles)
